The Sakura Chicken
Difference: Why it is
Naturally Better

Quality in food is often best understood through experience. From texture to flavour, the difference becomes clear with every bite. Sakura Chicken is raised with a focus on consistency and care, ensuring that each product meets a high standard.

It is produced under a No Antibiotics Ever approach, supported by lactobacillus-based feeding and controlled farming environments. These practices contribute to meat that is clean, safe, and consistently tender. Different cuts serve different purposes in the kitchen. Chicken breast offers a lean and versatile option, suitable for grilling or salads. Thigh meat provides a richer texture, making it ideal for braising or stir-frying. Drumsticks are well-suited for roasting, while a whole chicken creates a complete and satisfying meal for families or gatherings.

Sakura Chicken is trusted by both professional chefs and home cooks for its reliability and quality. When the ingredient is right, the cooking process becomes more intuitive, and the results more rewarding.
