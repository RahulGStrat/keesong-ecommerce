Steps to ordering on
Kee Song Online

Enjoy a seamless checkout experience!

Step 1:

Add your desired products
to cart selecting quantity
you wish to purchase.

Step 2:

Review your order by
clicking on your basket.

Step 3:

Key in your delivery information. You may apply promo code
or utilise any Sakura Voyage membership points here.

Step 4:

Select your preferred
delivery date and timing.

Step 5:

Select your payment mode
and enter payment details.

Step 6:

Before confirming your purchase, read through the Terms & Conditions and
tick the box for agreement.
