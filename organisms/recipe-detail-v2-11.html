Recipe

Diabetic-Friendly
Lemon Chicken

A light and refreshing dish made with tender chicken cooked in a zesty lemon-based sauce. Designed to be lower in sugar while still flavourful, it’s a healthier option without compromising on taste.

Prep Time:
10 mins

Cook Time:
20 mins

Serves:
2–3

INGREDIENTS

To marinate the chicken

  1. Chicken drumsticks (skin off).

Aromatics (to be finely chopped/pounded)

  1. 3 cloves of garlic, minced.

Everything else

  1. Fresh lemon juice.
  2. Olive oil.
  3. Salt and pepper to taste.
  4. Fresh herbs (thyme or rosemary).

STEPS

  1. Pre-heat oven to 190 degrees.
  2. Combine lemon juice, olive oil, minced garlic, salt, and pepper in a bowl.
  3. Place chicken drumsticks in a baking dish and pour lemon marinade over them.
  4. Sprinkle fresh herbs on top for added flavour.
  5. Bake for 25–30 minutes or until the chicken is cooked through and golden brown.
  6. Season with salt and pepper according to taste.
  7. Serve hot and enjoy.
