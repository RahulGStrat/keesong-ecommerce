Recipe

Garlic Butter Chicken
& Creamy Potatoes

A hearty and indulgent dish where tender chicken is cooked in a rich garlic butter sauce, paired with soft, creamy potatoes. Perfect for a comforting home-cooked meal with a satisfying, well-rounded flavour.

Prep Time:
15 mins

Cook Time:
40 mins

Serves:
3–4

INGREDIENTS

Aromatics (to be finely chopped/pounded)

  1. 400g raw chicken breast cut into cubes.
  2. 1 tablespoon of minced garlic.
  3. 800g potatoes cut into cubes.

Everything else

  1. 250ml light evaporated milk.
  2. 150g garlic herb cream cheese.
  3. 1 teaspoon of salt.
  4. 2 teaspoons of parsley.
  5. 2 teaspoons of garlic powder.
  6. 2 teaspoons of chilli flakes.
  7. 2 teaspoons of onion powder.
  8. 2 teaspoons of paprika.
  9. 2 teaspoons of olive oil.
  10. 50g of butter.

STEPS

  1. Cook the chicken on medium-high heat for 6–8 minutes until golden brown.
  2. Lower the heat when adding the butter, minced garlic, and parsley. Mix for 1 minute, then set aside.
  3. When adding the milk, ensure that the pan is on medium-low heat.
  4. Stir continuously for about 5 minutes until it bubbles and thickens, then add the cooked potatoes.
