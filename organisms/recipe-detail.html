Recipe

Jamie Oliver's
Chicken in milk
(simplified)

INGREDIENTS

To marinate the chicken

  1. Sakura Whole Chicken.
  2. 2 teaspoons of salt and black pepper.

Everything else

  1. 100g unsalted butter.
  2. A drizzle of olive oil.
  3. 2 lemons (skin/zest only).
  4. 1 bunch of fresh basil.
  5. 1 whole garlic bulb.
  6. 800ml milk (any fat %).

STEPS

  1. Remove the chicken head if needed, then wash and pat dry.
  2. Season the chicken generously with salt and black pepper.
  3. Heat butter with olive oil and brown evenly.
  4. Add lemon zest, garlic and basil.
  5. Pour milk until it surrounds the chicken.
  6. Transfer pot into oven (190°C).
  7. Bake for 90 minutes.
  8. Remove, season and serve warm.
