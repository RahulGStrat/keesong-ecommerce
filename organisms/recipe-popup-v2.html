Recipe

Chicken in Milk
(simplified)

Inspired by a well-known chicken in milk recipe popularised by chefs like Jamie Oliver, this version is adapted for home cooking using Sakura Chicken.

Prep Time:
15 mins

Cook Time:
90 mins

Serves:
3–4

INGREDIENTS

To marinate the chicken

  1. Sakura Whole Chicken.
  2. 2 teaspoons of salt and black pepper.

Everything else

  1. 100g unsalted butter.
  2. a drizzle of olive oil.
  3. 2 lemons (skin/zest only).
  4. 1 bunch of fresh basil.
  5. 1 whole garlic bulb
    (separate cloves, skin intact).
  6. 800ml milk (any fat %).

STEPS

  1. Remove the chicken head if needed, then wash and pat dry thoroughly.
  2. Season the chicken generously with salt and black pepper.
  3. Heat butter with a little olive oil in a pot over medium heat. Brown the chicken on all sides until evenly golden.
  4. Once browned, add in the lemon zest, garlic cloves (with skin on) and fresh basil.
  5. Pour in the milk until it soaks the chicken.
  6. Preheat oven to 190°C. Transfer the pot into the oven (uncovered).
  7. Bake for 90 minutes, occasionally spooning the sauce over the chicken if needed.
  8. Remove from oven, check seasoning and serve warm with the creamy sauce.
