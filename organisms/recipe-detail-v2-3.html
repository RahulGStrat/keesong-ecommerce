Recipe

Salt Baked
Herbal Chicken

A traditional herbal chicken infused with Chinese medicinal ingredients like Dang Gui and Dang Shen. Slow-baked in a sealed wrap, the chicken remains juicy while absorbing deep, aromatic flavours.

Prep Time:
15 mins
(excluding
overnight
marination)

Cook Time:
90 mins

Serves:
2–3

INGREDIENTS

To marinate the chicken

  1. ½ Sakura Chicken.
  2. 2 tsp salt (or enough to rub all over the chicken).
  3. 1 tsp light soy sauce.
  4. 1 tbsp shao xing hua tiao cooking wine.
  5. 1 tsp sesame oil.
  6. ½ tsp white pepper.
  7. 1 tsp corn flour.

Aromatics (to be finely chopped/pounded)

  1. 6 red dates, halved.
  2. 6 slices angelica sinensis (dang gui, rehydrated).
  3. 6 slices codonopsis root (dang shen, rehydrated).

Everything else

  1. 1 tbsp wolfberries.

STEPS

  1. Rub the chicken thoroughly with salt, light soy sauce, Shao Xing wine, sesame oil, white pepper and corn flour. Marinate overnight in the fridge for best flavour.
  2. When ready to cook, combine the chicken with red dates, Dang Gui, Dang Shen, wolfberries, and a little of the reserved herbal soaking water.
  3. Wrap the chicken tightly in baking paper. Let the chicken return to room temperature before baking.
  4. Bake at 180°C for 90 minutes (longer if the chicken is bigger). Remove from the oven and let it rest for 15 minutes before unwrapping and serving.
