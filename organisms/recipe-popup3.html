Recipe

Roasted Mandarin
Orange Chicken

INGREDIENTS

Ingredients:

Main

  1. 1 Whole Sakura Chicken.

Aromatics (to be finely chopped/pounded)

  1. mandarin orange juice
  2. ginger (finely chopped or grated)
  3. garlic (finely chopped)
  4. orange zest

Everything else

  1. yuzu honey
  2. chicken essence
  3. butter
  4. plain flour

STEPS

  1. Combine mandarin orange juice, yuzu honey, chicken essence, butter, plain flour, ginger, garlic and orange zest to form a sauce.
  2. Mix well until the sauce is smooth and slightly thickened.
  3. Coat the chicken generously with the sauce, ensuring it is evenly covered.
  4. Preheat oven to 200°C and roast for 10 minutes.
  5. Reduce temperature to 180°C and continue roasting for 35 minutes.
  6. Remove from oven, let it rest slightly, and serve warm.
