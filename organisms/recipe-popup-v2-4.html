Recipe

Braised Chicken
with Abalone

A hearty claypot dish featuring Sakura Whole Chicken braised together with premium baby abalone, mushrooms and vegetables. Rich, savoury and deeply flavourful, it's perfect for family meals or special occasions.

Prep Time:
20 mins
(excluding
marinating)

Cook Time:
40 mins

Serves:
3–4

INGREDIENTS

To marinate the chicken

  1. 1 whole Sakura Chicken.
  2. 1 tbsp light soy sauce.
  3. 1 tbsp premium oyster sauce.
  4. 1 tbsp shaoxing wine.
  5. 1/2 tbsp dark soy sauce.
  6. 1/2 tbsp sesame oil.
  7. pepper.

Option stuffing: some spring onion, garlic & ginger

Everything else

  1. 1 can braised baby abalone.
  2. 1 pack wawa chye (250g).
  3. 150g fresh shitake mushroom.
  4. 12 quail eggs.
  5. 4 garlic.
  6. 4 shallots.
  7. 100g spring onion.
  8. 1 tbsp shaoxing wine.
  9. spring onion & chili (for garnish)

STEPS

  1. Marinate the whole chicken with all marinade ingredients for at least 3 hours or overnight. Stuff the cavity with spring onion, garlic and ginger if using.
  2. Air fry the marinated chicken at 180°C for 20 minutes until lightly browned.
  3. Heat a claypot or cast iron pot with a little oil. Pre-fry WaWa chye and mushrooms briefly, then set aside.
  4. Add a small amount of sesame oil into the pot. Place garlic, shallots, spring onion and WaWa chye at the base.
  5. Place the half-cooked chicken on top. Add Shaoxing wine, the whole can of braised abalone with sauce, quail eggs and mushrooms.
  6. Cover and simmer on low heat for 20 minutes until the chicken is fully cooked and flavours are absorbed.
  7. Garnish with chopped spring onion and chilli. Drizzle hot oil over before serving.
