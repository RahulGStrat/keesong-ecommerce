Recipe

Herbal
Chicken Pot
(No Water Added)

INGREDIENTS

Ingredients:

Main

  1. 1 Whole Sakura Chicken.

Everything else

  1. 1 red apple
  2. 1 yellow onion
  3. 1 medium carrot
  4. 3–4 napa cabbage leaves
  5. ½ daikon
  6. 1 teaspoon sea salt
  7. 5 red dates
  8. ½ tablespoon goji berries
  9. Spring onion, chopped (for garnish)

STEPS

  1. Prepare and cut all vegetables into large chunks.
  2. Layer the vegetables, red dates and goji berries evenly at the bottom of the pot.
  3. Place the whole Sakurako Chicken in the centre.
  4. Sprinkle sea salt over the ingredients.
  5. Cover with a tight lid and cook on low heat for 1 hour.
  6. No water is needed as the ingredients will release natural juices during cooking.
  7. Optional: Add up to 1 cup of water if you prefer more broth.
  8. Garnish with chopped spring onion before serving.

Cooking Tip:

Use fresh chicken and cook in a cast iron or clay pot with a tight lid for a clean, rich and concentrated broth.
