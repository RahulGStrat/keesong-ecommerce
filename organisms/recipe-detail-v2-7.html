Recipe

Soy Marinated
Chicken with Rice

A simple yet satisfying dish featuring chicken marinated in a savoury soy-based sauce, then cooked until tender and juicy. Served with rice, it’s a comforting everyday meal packed with rich umami flavours.

Prep Time:
10 mins
(excluding
marination)

Cook Time:
30 mins

Serves:
2–3

Marinating: ideally at least 2–4 hours

INGREDIENTS

To marinate the chicken

  1. Chicken.
  2. Salt & pepper.
  3. Sesame oil.
  4. Soy sauce.
  5. Sugar.

Aromatics (to be finely chopped/pounded)

  1. Spring onions.

Everything else

  1. Rice.
  2. Eggs.
  3. Sesame seeds.

STEPS

  1. Season chicken with salt & pepper.
  2. Marinate it with soy sauce, sesame oil & a pinch of sugar.
  3. Pan fry the chicken till slightly charred.
  4. Scramble eggs.
  5. Assemble on rice and garnish with spring onions and sesame seeds.
